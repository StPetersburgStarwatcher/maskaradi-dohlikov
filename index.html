    More Merits, at a price

    An optional rule from Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary Edition (p. 82), converted to V5: you may take more Flaws than you have to, and every extra Flaw point buys one more point of Merits.

    • You still take the usual Flaws first; only points beyond them count.
    • Flaws are capped at 7 points in all, so you can gain at most a few extra points.
    • The extra points buy Merits only, not Backgrounds.
    • Your Storyteller has the last word on which Flaws make sense for the chronicle.

    Switch to Free Edit?

    This is a mode for Storytellers and those who are already in the game. If this is your first time building this character, we recommend you continue with a regular character creation.

    Free Edit lifts the creation rules: dots go up to 5, Disciplines and powers aren’t limited by the starting spread, and budgets and the to-do list are switched off. Experience isn’t used: anything already bought with it becomes part of the character. You can switch back at any time.
